❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q Is the license shared with the Windows version?
A Yes, the license is universal. A license purchased for the Windows version works on the Linux version as well.
Q I lost my license key
A Please provide the email address used at purchase. We will resend your license information. Contact us at support@mojosoft.co.jp.
Q We're considering enterprise deployment
A We offer a business license with unlimited PC usage. Please contact us for details.
Q Can I get a refund?
A We offer a 30-day free trial, so please try the app thoroughly before purchasing. Refunds are generally not available, but please contact us if you have special circumstances.
Q It doesn't work on my Linux distribution
A Please report the issue on GitHub Issues with your distribution name, version, and error messages. We will investigate.

🔧 When Reporting Bugs

Including the following information helps us resolve issues more quickly:

  • OS: Distribution name and version (e.g., Ubuntu 22.04 LTS)
  • Architecture: x86_64 or aarch64
  • Desktop environment: GNOME, KDE, etc. / X11 or Wayland
  • RocketWhisper version: Check in the Settings screen
  • Symptoms: What happened and what you expected
  • Steps to reproduce: How to reproduce the issue
  • Error messages: Any error messages displayed
